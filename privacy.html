Privacy

What BALL keeps about you, why, and how to take it back. Last updated 21 October 2026.

What we keep

Your email address (for signing in only; nobody else sees it), your username, your club's design, the club and national team you support and the country you live in (if you picked them), your game stats (matches, rating, XP, titles, leagues, answers to questions), and the reports you sent.

If you sign in with Google, we receive your email address and nothing else from your Google account.

Why

To keep your club and your progress across phones, to run leagues with your friends, to rate how hard each question is, and to keep names in leagues clean.

Who sees what

League mates see your club name, crest, kit, scarf, @username, level and rating. Under 16, others see you as "Player NNNN" and your club stays private. Reports are anonymous.

Emails

Only the sign-in link, unless you ticked "Tell me about new modes". You can switch that off any time in Settings.

Your data, your call

Export everything from Settings → Export my data. Delete your account in Settings: that removes your profile, club, matches, leagues and reports from the server.

Where

Stored with Supabase, our database provider. Guests' games stay on their own phone and are never sent. We don't sell your data and there are no ads or trackers.
