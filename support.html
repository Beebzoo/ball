Support

The questions that come up most, and how to reach us. Last updated 2 November 2026.

Questions

How do I install BALL as an app?

On Android, open the browser menu (the three dots, or the address bar) and pick Install app or Add to home screen. On an iPhone, in Safari, tap Share (the square with the arrow) and then Add to Home Screen. BALL gets its own icon, opens full screen and works offline too. Nothing to download from a store.

I lost my progress, or I have a new phone.

As a guest, everything is saved on your phone only, so clearing the browser's data or changing phones leaves it behind. Found your club (from the home screen or Settings) saves your career on the server: sign in on the new phone with the same email or Google account and it all comes back. If you founded your club and still miss something, write to us with your username.

A question is wrong.

Under every revealed answer there is a line, Something wrong with this question? Tap it, pick the reason and add a note if you like. We read the reports every week, fix the question in the bank and push the fix with the next build. Thirty thousand questions, one fan: your report is how they get right.

How do leagues and codes work?

In Leagues, make a league and you get a six-character code. Share the code with your friends; they type it in and they are in (up to 30 in a league, and you can be in several). Every week closes on Sunday night, Amsterdam time, with a table of the week. You need a club, a username and to be 16 or older. Call It leagues work the same way, with their own code.

When does Call It lock?

Each match on a card locks at its own kick-off, on the server, so there is no calling a goal you have just watched. Your calls stay hidden from your league mates until then, and are shown after. For cup matches, the score after 90 minutes counts; extra time and penalties don't.

I'm under 16. What can I do?

Everything in the game: every quiz, the Daily and your streaks, your own club with its crest and scarf, XP, levels and trophies. Friends, leagues and a public username are off until you are 16, and other players see you as "Player NNNN". We ask your age once, as a yes or no, and never ask for a birth date. The privacy page has the why.

How do I delete my account?

Settings → Delete my account. That removes your profile, club, matches, leagues, Call It calls and reports from the server; it can't be undone. What is on your phone stays until you clear it. Your name also disappears from your old leagues' closed weeks.

How do I export my data?

Settings → Export my data downloads one file with everything, from this phone and from the server: your profile, club, matches, leagues, Call It calls and the reports you sent. It is plain JSON, yours to keep.

What are reminders, and how do I switch them off?

If you turn them on in Settings, BALL can send you one reminder a day at most: your streak at risk in the evening, or your Call It card about to lock. Switch them off in Settings, or in your browser's or phone's notification settings for the site. They are off unless you turn them on.

Is BALL official?

No. BALL is a football quiz made by one fan and is not affiliated with any club, league, UEFA or FIFA. Club and player names in questions describe facts; the facts come in part from Wikidata (CC0). The crests, kits and badges you see are our own.
