What BALL is

A free football quiz, Call It, a prediction league you play with your friends, and DART and RACKET, a darts game and a tennis game you reach from BALL. Both are played for glory and nothing else. There are no prizes and no money anywhere in it: you cannot pay anything in, and there is nothing to be won but a place in the table and the right to say you called it. A score you call is a score you call; it is never anything more.

If BALL ever sells anything (a Supporter badge, say: cosmetics only, never an advantage in the game), a separate note will cover it.

Your own club, not a real one

Found a club of your own. Don't copy real clubs, players or people: their names, crests or kits. Support whoever you like; just don't pretend to be them.

Names you'd shout in a stadium

Nothing offensive, hateful or rude, in your club name, your username or the name on your shirt. If enough players report a name we hide it until we've had a look, and we may rename your club. You can always ask for a second look.

Play it straight

No looking answers up during league or online matches. Your friends are trusting you. We may remove results that clearly weren't played straight.

Leagues are for friends: you make one, get a code, and share it with the people you want in it. You may be in several. Everyone in a league sees everyone else's club and results. A league is as kind as the people in it; if one goes wrong, leave it.

Moderation, and a second look

A name reported by enough players is hidden while we look; the owner sees "A new name, please" and others see "Club NNNN" until it is sorted. We look every week. If a name breaks these rules we ask for a new one; we never ban anyone quietly for a name. If you think we got it wrong, ask for a second look: in the app, or by writing to hello@ballquiz.app. One person reads it and answers.

Someone who keeps breaking these rules after being asked to stop may lose their account.

Your account

One account per person. You can play without one; a club saves your career across phones. You can delete your account in Settings at any time; that removes your profile, club, matches, leagues, calls and reports from the server. We can close accounts that break these rules, and we may remove accounts nobody has used for a very long time, after letting you know by email where we can.

Under 16 you can play everything and keep your club, but friends, leagues and a public username are off. The privacy page says why.

Availability

BALL is offered as it is, by one fan, for free. It may be down now and then, it will change, and one day it may stop. We try to say so in the app when something big changes. You can export your data at any time, in Settings, so nothing of yours is locked in.

Club and player names in questions are used to describe those facts, not to suggest a link. Player and club facts come in part from Wikidata (CC0). BALL is not affiliated with any club, league, UEFA or FIFA.

Your club's name and design are yours to use; you give us the right to show them in the game, which is what they are for.
